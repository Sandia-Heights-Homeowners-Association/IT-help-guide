# Volunteer Access

Use this page for new volunteers and role holders who need to understand what
they should expect from Microsoft 365.

## External Volunteers

Most volunteers do not get an `@sandiahomeowners.org` mailbox. They use their
personal email address and receive committee messages through Microsoft Groups.

First-time setup:

1. Find the Microsoft organization invitation email.
2. Click **Accept invitation**.
3. Sign in with the same personal email address that received the invitation.
4. Open the committee SharePoint link from the directory.
5. Confirm you receive messages from the committee mailing list.

External volunteers use SharePoint in their web browser for committee
documents. Do not add committee folders to OneDrive or move committee files
into a personal OneDrive.

The invitation may mention Teams. For normal SHHA volunteer work, use email and
SharePoint links instead of Teams.

## Expected Invitation Emails

You may see two Microsoft emails:

- Organization invitation: the important one. It usually says SHHA invited you
  to collaborate. Accept this invitation.
- Group or team notification: default Microsoft wording. It may mention Teams.
  You can ignore the Teams wording unless staff specifically asks you to use it.

If Microsoft lands you on a confusing page after acceptance, use the direct
links in [Email, Files, and Directory](02-email-files-directory.md).

## Role Inboxes For Officers

Only these six officer roles have shared mailboxes:

- `president@sandiahomeowners.org`
- `vicepresident@sandiahomeowners.org`
- `secretary@sandiahomeowners.org`
- `treasurer@sandiahomeowners.org`
- `accchair@sandiahomeowners.org`
- `cscchair@sandiahomeowners.org`

Other committee chairs and task-force leads do **not** get shared mailboxes.
They use their own account and the committee mailing list.

An officer may also be included in a committee group using another email
address, but shared-mailbox access always uses the officer's SHHA account.

These addresses belong to the role, not the person. You do not sign in directly
as the role address. Access is granted to your own licensed
`@sandiahomeowners.org` account with **Full Access** and **Send As** permission.

The mailbox will not necessarily appear in Outlook automatically. After the
office tells you access is ready, open Outlook and add it manually:

1. In Outlook on the web, right-click **Folders** and choose **Add shared folder
   or mailbox**, then enter the role address.
2. In new Outlook, right-click **Shared with me** and choose **Add shared folder
   or mailbox**. In classic Outlook, use **Account Settings** → **Change** →
   **More Settings** → **Advanced** → **Add**.

Microsoft's current instructions are in [Open and use a shared mailbox in
Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/sharing/open-and-use-a-shared-mailbox-in-outlook).

You cannot log in directly to a shared mailbox. Sign in to Outlook with your
own SHHA account, then open the role mailbox from there. For important official
correspondence, either send from the role mailbox or copy the role mailbox so
the message becomes part of the role's history.

Best practice: reply from the role inbox, or copy it, when handling official
officer business so future role holders can see the history.

## Why The President Has Two Email Addresses

Short version: `robin.otten@sandiahomeowners.org` is Robin's personal SHHA
account. `president@sandiahomeowners.org` is the President mailbox. One belongs
to the person; one belongs to the office.

| Address | What it is | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `robin.otten@sandiahomeowners.org` | Robin's own SHHA sign-in and personal work account | Signing in to Microsoft, opening Outlook/SharePoint, and messages meant specifically for Robin as a person |
| `president@sandiahomeowners.org` | The shared President inbox | Official President business that future Presidents may need to see later |

Yes, two inboxes is annoying. Think of it as a key and a filing cabinet:
Robin's account is the key that lets her into Microsoft; the President inbox is
the filing cabinet that stays with the office when the next President takes
over.

Simple rule: if it is official President business, use
`president@sandiahomeowners.org`. If it is about Robin personally, use
`robin.otten@sandiahomeowners.org`. If you are not sure, the President inbox is
usually the safer mailbox for SHHA business.

## File Sharing Pattern

For committee documents:

1. Upload the file to the committee SharePoint site.
2. Note the folder location and file name.
3. Email that information to the committee mailing-list address.

Avoid sending attachments for documents that should remain in committee records.
Do not use **Share** or **Copy link** to grant the group access; committee access
comes from membership in the Microsoft 365 group.

## When To Ask For Help

Contact `ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org` if:

- You accepted the invitation but still cannot open SharePoint.
- You are not receiving committee group email.
- A shared role inbox does not appear in Outlook.
- You get an access-denied message.

Include the email address you are using, the SharePoint link or mailbox involved,
and a screenshot of the error if possible.
