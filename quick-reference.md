# Quick Reference

Use this page for the essentials only.

## If You Remember Only 5 Things

1. Accept your Microsoft invitation email first.
2. Use SharePoint links, not file attachments, for committee docs.
3. Do not share files directly to committee groups for external-member committees.
4. If you have a role inbox, reply as that role (or CC it) for SHHA business.
5. Contact **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** when stuck.

## Core Access Points

- **SharePoint links**: primary location for committee files — use the direct link from your chair or ITHelp
- **Outlook on the web** ([outlook.office.com](https://outlook.office.com)): used by staff and role mailbox holders to read/send email
- **Committee group addresses** (e.g., `csc@sandiahomeowners.org`): email these to reach your entire committee
- **Google Calendar reminders**: used for Board scheduling notifications
- **SHHA website** ([sandiahomeowners.org](https://sandiahomeowners.org)): public committee pages, community directory

## Common Actions

### Share a file with your committee

1. Upload file to SharePoint
2. Copy SharePoint link
3. Email link to committee mailing list

### Confirm your access setup

- Confirm invitation was accepted
- Confirm you can open the committee SharePoint link
- Confirm you receive committee group emails

### Send email to your committee or Board

- Compose a normal email to the group address (e.g., `csc@sandiahomeowners.org`)
- See [FAQ — Sending Email to a Group](faq.html#how-do-i-send-an-email-to-a-specific-group-board-committee-or-task-force) for the full address list

### Find meeting minutes or documents

- Open your committee's SharePoint site → Documents → look for a "Minutes" folder
- See [FAQ — Finding Minutes](faq.html#where-do-i-find-minutes-from-committee-or-board-meetings)

### Submit a GRIT article

- Email your article to the GRIT editor (ask staff or your chair for the current editor's email)
- See [FAQ — GRIT Submissions](faq.html#how-do-i-submit-an-article-for-the-grit-newsletter)

### Request an email blast to residents

- Email your draft to **office@sandiahomeowners.org**
- See [FAQ — Email Blasts](faq.html#how-do-i-send-an-email-blast-to-all-shha-residents)

## Contact
