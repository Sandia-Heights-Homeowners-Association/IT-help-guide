Staff and authorized volunteers: SHHA Toolbox

The SHHA Toolbox brings office and committee work into one Microsoft-authenticated site. Sign in to the toolbox with the Microsoft identity invited to SHHA. An invited guest can use a personal email address; an SHHA email suffix is not required. Each private tool needs its own approved access. If a tool is missing or access is denied, contact IT Help or office staff.

The public dashboard, map, submissions site and wiki are separate destinations. You do not need toolbox access to use their public pages. The GRIT editorial workspace has its own protected access; toolbox Microsoft login does not unlock it.

Toolbox functionWhat it helps authorized staff do
Email RemindersSet reminder rules, recipient groups and templates for events already on the Microsoft 365 calendar. Change events in Outlook or Teams, then sync the calendar in the tool.
Group update emailerWatch selected Microsoft 365 groups and notify a group when its membership changes. Microsoft 365 remains the membership source.
SHHA addresses that need attentionReview contact gaps, membership and mailing-address issues in a private staff workspace.
Printed DirectoryPrepare owner listings and printable sections while honoring current directory-listing preferences.
Membership reconciliationCompare SHS billing information with website records, retain review notes and produce private issue reports. Suggestions need review; the toolbox does not write corrections to the website database.
Bulk emailerPrepare personalized office messages, review recipients and arrange paced campaigns. Sender configuration and approval are separate from preparing a message.
Access and Operational statusAdministrators manage tool grants and inspect configuration, sending/worker status and backups.

Member Information remains a future workspace; it is not a general homeowner lookup. Private resident information, reports and submissions must stay in their protected systems.

Meeting reminders: use the toolbox

The former standalone reminders address is retired. Open the toolbox and choose Email Reminders. See SHHA Reminders for the staff workflow. Microsoft 365 owns the calendar; the tool reads it and sends configured notifications.
