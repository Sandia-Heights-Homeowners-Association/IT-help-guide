GRIT content rules and submission guide

For residents, neighbors, Board and committee members, office staff, and anyone contributing to the GRIT. Start by choosing the section that fits your contribution, then submit it through the GRIT Submission Portal.

Approved rules, in a reader-friendly format. This page presents the GRIT Content Rules & Submission Guide approved by the Communications & Publications Committee on June 22, 2026. The original approved PDF is available for reference.

Start here

  1. Choose your section. Use the guide below to decide whether your submission is a community contribution, classified, local event, or advertisement.
  2. Prepare your content. Check the rules for that section. For photos and graphics, check that details remain clear in black and white.
  3. Submit through the portal. Open the GRIT Submission Portal and select the category that best matches your content. If you are unsure, choose the closest match; editors can reassign it.

Need help or unable to use the portal? Email your submission or questions to the GRIT editor at griteditor@sandiahomeowners.org.

Plan for the next issue. The SHHA GRIT issues page asks for submissions before the 20th of the month for the following month's issue, which appears on or about the 1st. This timing is additional information from the website; the June 22 rules do not specify a deadline. Publication in a particular issue is not guaranteed.

Keep it informative, constructive, and neighborly

The GRIT welcomes content that educates, celebrates community involvement, shares useful information, and helps residents connect with one another.

Articles work best when they explain an issue, provide context, share an experience, or offer a solution. Content focused mainly on criticism, complaints, or repeated reminders is less likely to be effective. Editors may edit it for tone and clarity, or it may fail editorial review.

Community Contributions

This is the main section for resident-submitted content. Most submissions received through the portal are considered for this section unless another section is more appropriate.

Residents, Board members, committee members, office staff, and other community contributors may submit here when writing in a personal capacity, rather than on behalf of SHHA or an SHHA committee.

Examples include:

  • Opinions and commentary.
  • Educational content and personal experiences relevant to the community.
  • Local history, nature, and wildlife observations.
  • Neighbor appreciation and community announcements.
  • Other topics likely to interest Sandia Heights residents.

Whose views are these? Community Contributions reflect the author's views. They do not necessarily represent SHHA, its Board of Directors, committees, employees, or volunteers.

Classifieds

Classifieds give residents and local businesses a simple, equitable way to share brief announcements. Examples include services offered, items for sale, lost and found, requests for assistance, lessons or tutoring, pet sitting, babysitting, handyman services, and similar personal or small-business notices.

A submission is not a standing order. Classifieds must be resubmitted for each month in which publication is desired. For business-related listings, the once-per-calendar-quarter limit still applies.

Local Events

Use this section for upcoming community activities and public-interest events, such as gatherings, educational programs, workshops, performances, fundraisers, meetups, and similar public events.

  • Who can submit: residents and organizations with a physical presence in the Sandia Heights area, or events taking place within Sandia Heights that interest the Sandia Heights community.
  • Include the essentials: date, time, and location when applicable.
  • Submit again for another month: listings must be resubmitted each month if continued publication is desired.

Recurring events are not automatically republished. Commercial advertising, promotional content, and business marketing disguised as event listings are not permitted in Local Events.

Advertising

Any submission that includes images, logos, extended promotional text, or marketing-style content may be treated as paid advertising.

For advertising inquiries, contact the SHHA office at office@sandiahomeowners.org.

Choose photos and graphics that work in print

Photos and graphics are encouraged and can bring a story to life. The GRIT appears in color online and is mailed in black and white to more than 1,800 Sandia Heights households each month.

  1. Choose high-resolution images. Use images with enough detail to look clear when printed.
  2. Check important details in black and white. Make sure readers can still see the subject, labels, and other information.
  3. Use enough contrast. Text and important details should stand out from the background.
  4. Do not rely on color alone. A label or highlight that works in color may disappear when printed.

Example from the approved guide: the yellow callout works in color but is hard to see in black and white. A different color choice for the callout box and text can make the printed version readable.

For more practical writing and photo advice, see the GRIT Contributor Guide.

Send your submission

  1. Open the GRIT Submission Portal. This is the preferred submission route. It helps editors track content, communicate with contributors, manage revisions, and route submissions to the right section.
  2. Select the best-fitting category and submit your content. If you are unsure, choose the closest category; editors may move your submission to a more appropriate section.
  3. If you cannot use the portal, email the editor. Send your submission to griteditor@sandiahomeowners.org.

What happens after you submit?

  • Editorial review: editors may edit for clarity, length, style, formatting, and available space. Their goal is to preserve your voice and intent.
  • Substantive changes: if significant changes are proposed, editors will make a good-faith effort to provide the revised version for your review and approval.
  • Minor changes: grammar, style, formatting, and changes for space constraints may be made without additional review.
  • Issue placement: publication in a particular issue cannot be guaranteed. Depending on space, timing, and editorial needs, content may appear in a future issue, be abbreviated, or occasionally be declined.

For complete details about publication, editing, and contributor rights, read the GRIT Submission Terms & Conditions (PDF).
