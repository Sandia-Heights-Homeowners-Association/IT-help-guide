## Review order

1. Decide which information should be public and which should be restricted.
   All existing pages currently inherit public viewing. Start with Staff,
   GRIT Internal Production Reference, Systems Inventory, Controls & Security,
   and HR & Personnel Management. Titles alone do not prove sensitivity; read
   them and propose decisions. The public Git mirror retains old content in
   history, so restrict pages before adding sensitive material and choose a
   private backup destination separately.
2. Review the visitor journey: Overview, Quick Links, volunteer quick start,
   invited-guest instructions, email/groups and SharePoint guides. Verify the
   next step, reader audience, meaningful links and help contact on each page.
3. Check role guides, onboarding templates and Microsoft 365 procedures against
   current actual organization configuration. Microsoft owns mail/calendars/
   groups; the toolbox reads/uses them. Guest access is based on the SHHA tenant
   guest object, not a required SHHA email suffix or paid license.
4. Review reminders/toolbox references carefully. The DigitalOcean reminders
   sender is still live; do not describe its migration as complete or operate it.
5. Reconcile old guide/ edits and pending drafts individually against current
   wiki content. Some may be obsolete or already represented. No bulk replay.
6. Test public reading, member/guest editing and named edit attribution on the
   production hostname. Confirm SharePoint authorization independently: public
   wiki links do not grant access to committee documents.
