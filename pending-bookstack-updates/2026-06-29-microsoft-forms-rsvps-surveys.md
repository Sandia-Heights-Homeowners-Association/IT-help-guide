## New Page Body

```html
<p><strong>Audience:</strong> SHHA committee chairs, Board members, staff, and volunteers who need to collect a headcount, RSVP, poll, or survey response.</p>

<h2>Start Here</h2>
<p>For most SHHA forms, contact the office and ask them to create the form for you.</p>
<p>That keeps ownership with SHHA instead of one volunteer's personal Forms account, and it gives office staff a predictable place to find the response link and results later. Tiny convenience now beats a future scavenger hunt through old email.</p>

<h2>Common Uses</h2>
<ul>
<li>RSVPs for events, meetings, or volunteer activities</li>
<li>Headcounts for planning food, seating, handouts, or attendance</li>
<li>Short committee polls</li>
<li>Resident or volunteer surveys</li>
<li>Collecting simple contact or preference information</li>
</ul>

<h2>If You Have An SHHA Microsoft Account</h2>
<p>If you have an <code>@sandiahomeowners.org</code> email address and are comfortable owning the form, you can create one directly:</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to <a href="https://forms.microsoft.com">forms.microsoft.com</a>. You can also sign in at <a href="https://office.com">office.com</a>, open the app launcher, and choose <strong>Forms</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>New Form</strong>.</li>
<li>Add a clear title and short description.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>Add new</strong> to add questions. Common choices are <strong>Choice</strong>, <strong>Text</strong>, <strong>Rating</strong>, and <strong>Date</strong>.</li>
<li>Use <strong>Preview</strong> and submit one test response.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>Collect responses</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose who can respond, then copy the response link.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Choosing Who Can Respond</h2>
<p>For anything that includes residents, committee members using personal email addresses, or other external users, use a public response link such as <strong>Anyone can respond</strong>.</p>
<p>If you need names, phone numbers, email addresses, committee names, or household information, ask for those as questions on the form. Microsoft's automatic <strong>record name</strong> and <strong>one response per person</strong> options only work well when everyone signs in with a supported Microsoft work or school account.</p>
<p>For an internal staff-only form, the office may choose a more restrictive setting.</p>

<h2>The Big Sharing Quirk</h2>
<p>A Microsoft Form is not shared like a Word document, PDF, or SharePoint file. It does not appear in someone else's OneDrive or document library just because you shared it.</p>
<p>People reach the form by using the link. Put the link somewhere easy to find, such as:</p>
<ul>
<li>The announcement email</li>
<li>A calendar invitation</li>
<li>The relevant committee SharePoint page or folder note</li>
<li>A wiki page</li>
<li>A reminder email</li>
</ul>
<p>If someone loses the link, they will need to find the original email or ask for the link again. This is normal Microsoft behavior, not a personal failing. Microsoft just likes to keep us humble.</p>

<h2>Do Not Mix Up These Two Links</h2>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Link type</th><th>Who gets it</th><th>What it does</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td><strong>Collect responses</strong></td><td>Residents, committee members, attendees, survey respondents</td><td>Lets people fill out the form</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Collaborate</strong></td><td>Office staff or another trusted editor</td><td>Lets people edit the form and view response data</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>Send the <strong>Collect responses</strong> link to the public or committee. Only send the <strong>Collaborate</strong> link to someone who should help administer the form.</p>

<h2>For Office Staff</h2>
<ol>
<li>Create SHHA business forms from an office or other SHHA-owned Microsoft account when possible.</li>
<li>Use a clear title, such as <strong>2026 Annual Meeting RSVP</strong> or <strong>ACC Volunteer Availability Survey</strong>.</li>
<li>Test the form before sending it widely.</li>
<li>If external users will respond, test with a non-SHHA email address.</li>
<li>Send the <strong>Collect responses</strong> link in the announcement email.</li>
<li>Save important results by exporting them to Excel and placing the file in the appropriate SharePoint document library.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Suggested Email Text</h2>
<p>Use this when sending a form link:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Please complete this short form by [DATE]:<br><a href="[FORM LINK]">[FORM LINK]</a></p>
<p>If the link does not open when clicked, copy and paste it into your browser.</p>
</blockquote>

<h2>Troubleshooting</h2>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Problem</th><th>What to do</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Someone cannot open the form</td><td>Check the response setting. If they are outside SHHA, the form likely needs an external/public response link.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Someone cannot find the form later</td><td>Resend the response link or put it somewhere persistent, such as SharePoint, a calendar item, or the wiki.</td></tr>
<tr><td>The wrong person owns the form</td><td>Export the results to Excel and save them in SharePoint. For recurring forms, recreate the next version from an SHHA-owned account.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Someone received an edit/collaboration link by mistake</td><td>Remove or regenerate the collaboration link, then send the correct <strong>Collect responses</strong> link.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2>Microsoft Help</h2>
<ul>

</ul>
```

## FAQ Addition

<hr>
<h2>How do I create a form for an RSVP, headcount, or survey?</h2>
<p>For most SHHA forms, contact the office and ask them to create the form for you. That keeps the form and response results with SHHA instead of tying them to one person's account.</p>
<p>If you have an <code>@sandiahomeowners.org</code> account, you can create a form at <a href="https://forms.microsoft.com">forms.microsoft.com</a> or through the Microsoft 365 app launcher. Use <strong>Collect responses</strong> to copy the link that respondents should use.</p>
<p>Important quirk: Forms are shared by link. They do not appear in someone else's OneDrive or SharePoint library like a document. Put the response link somewhere people can find again, such as an email, calendar item, SharePoint note, or wiki page.</p>
<p>See <a href="/books/shha-it-help-guide/page/microsoft-forms-for-rsvps-and-surveys">Microsoft Forms For RSVPs And Surveys</a> for the full walkthrough.</p>
