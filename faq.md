## I was invited but cannot access files. What is usually wrong?

The most common cause is that the original Microsoft invitation email was never accepted. Until you accept that invitation, Microsoft does not recognize you as an authorized guest, so SharePoint links and group emails will not work.

**How to fix it — step by step:**

1. **Search your email** (Gmail, Yahoo Mail, iCloud Mail, etc.) for a message from **"Microsoft Invitations"** with the subject line *"You've been invited to access applications within the Sandia Homeowners organization"* (or similar wording). Check your **Spam / Junk** folder — these invitations often land there.
2. **Open the email** and click the **"Accept invitation"** button or link.
3. **Sign in with your personal email address** — the same one the invitation was sent to. If you already have a Microsoft account linked to that email, use its password. If you do not have a Microsoft account, Microsoft will walk you through creating one (it is free) or will send you a one-time passcode to verify your identity.
4. **Review and accept the permissions prompt.** Microsoft will ask you to agree that SHHA can see basic profile information (your name and email). Click **Accept**.
5. **Try opening your SharePoint link again.** It should now work.

If you cannot find the invitation email or the link has expired, contact **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** and they can resend it.

> **Microsoft help:** [B2B collaboration invitation redemption](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/external-id/redemption-experience) explains the full invitation-acceptance process.

## Do I need a paid Microsoft license?

**Usually no.**

Most SHHA volunteers are added as **external guest users** in Microsoft 365. Guest access is free — you do not need to purchase Microsoft 365, and SHHA does not need to buy a license for you.

As a guest you can:
- Receive committee group emails at your personal email address
- Open and edit documents shared with you on SharePoint
- View files in your web browser without installing any software

The only people who need paid Microsoft 365 licenses are SHHA office staff and IT administrators.

> **Microsoft help:** [What is guest access in Microsoft 365 Groups?](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/adding-guests-to-microsoft-365-groups-bfc7a840-868f-4fd6-a390-f347bf51aff6) covers what guests can and cannot do.

## Do I need to create a new username or password?

**No.** Use the same personal email address that received your SHHA invitation (for example, your Gmail, Yahoo Mail, Outlook.com, or iCloud address).

- **If that email is already a Microsoft account** (e.g., any @outlook.com, @hotmail.com, or @live.com address, or a personal email you previously registered with Microsoft), sign in with its existing password.
- **If that email is not a Microsoft account**, Microsoft will either send you a **one-time passcode** by email each time you sign in, or prompt you to create a free Microsoft account. Either way, you do not need a separate SHHA-specific username or password.

> **Microsoft help:** [How to create a new Microsoft account](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/account-billing/how-to-create-a-new-microsoft-account-a84675c3-3e9e-17cf-2911-3d56b15c0aaf) — only needed if Microsoft asks you to create one during the invitation process.

## Can I share a file directly to the committee mailing list from SharePoint?

**Not reliably** when the committee group includes external (guest) members, which most SHHA groups do. SharePoint's built-in "Share" dialog sometimes cannot resolve external-member group addresses, and recipients may get "Access Denied" errors.

**Use this reliable method instead:**

1. **Upload your file to the committee's SharePoint document library.**
   - Open the SharePoint site link your committee uses.
   - Click **Upload** → **Files**, then select your file.
2. **Copy a sharing link for the file.**
   - Select the file (click the circle icon next to its name).
   - Click the **Share** button at the top of the page.
   - In the sharing dialog, click **Copy link**. Make sure the link setting says something like *"People in Sandia Homeowners with the link can edit"* (or *"can view"*). If you need to change this, click the gear icon next to "Copy link" to adjust permissions.
   - Click **Copy** to save the link to your clipboard.
3. **Email the link to the committee mailing list.**
   - Open your personal email (Gmail, Yahoo Mail, Outlook.com, etc.).
   - Compose a new message to the committee address (e.g., `csc@sandiahomeowners.org`).
   - Paste the SharePoint link into the email body. Add a brief description of the file.
   - Click **Send**.

> **Microsoft help:** [Share SharePoint files or folders](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/share-sharepoint-files-or-folders-1fe37332-0f9a-4719-970e-d2578da4941c) walks through the full sharing process with screenshots.

## Why should I send links instead of attachments?

Sending a SharePoint link instead of an email attachment is important because:

- **One official version.** Everyone views and edits the same file. No one ends up working on an outdated copy.
- **Less confusion.** There are no multiple versions floating around in different inboxes.
- **Centralized records.** The file stays in the committee's SharePoint library, where future volunteers and officers can find it.
- **No file-size limits.** Email attachments can bounce if they are too large. SharePoint links have no such problem.

**Rule of thumb:** If a document is SHHA committee business, it goes in SharePoint, and you email the link. Only use attachments for quick, one-off items that do not need to be retained (e.g., a casual photo).

> **Microsoft help:** [External or guest sharing in SharePoint](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/external-or-guest-sharing-in-onedrive-sharepoint-and-lists-7aa070b8-d094-4921-9dd9-86392f2a79e7) explains how sharing works when external guests are involved.

## I have a role mailbox (e.g., president@, treasurer@). Should I reply from my personal inbox?

**For SHHA business, always reply from the role mailbox** (e.g., `president@sandiahomeowners.org`, `treasurer@sandiahomeowners.org`), or at minimum **CC the role mailbox** so the message is saved there.

Why this matters: role mailboxes stay with the position, not the person. When leadership changes, the next officer inherits the full email history. If you replied only from your personal inbox, those records are lost to the organization.

**How to reply from the role mailbox using Outlook on the web:**

1. Go to [outlook.office.com](https://outlook.office.com) and sign in with your **SHHA licensed account** (your `@sandiahomeowners.org` address, not your personal email).
2. In the left sidebar, look under **"Shared with me"** (or right-click your account name and choose **"Add shared folder or mailbox"**, then type the role address, e.g., `president@sandiahomeowners.org`).
3. Open the email you want to reply to from the role mailbox's Inbox.
4. Click **Reply**. Check the **From** field — it should show the role address. If it shows your personal address instead, click **From** and select the role mailbox address.
5. Compose and click **Send**. The recipient will see the message came from the role address.

> **Microsoft help:** [Open and use a shared mailbox in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/open-and-use-a-shared-mailbox-in-outlook-d94a8e9e-21f1-4240-808b-de9c9c088afd) covers adding a shared mailbox and sending from it.

## I cannot see my shared role inbox in Outlook. What should I do?

If the shared mailbox does not appear automatically, try adding it manually:

**In Outlook on the web** ([outlook.office.com](https://outlook.office.com)):

1. Sign in with your SHHA `@sandiahomeowners.org` account.
2. In the left folder pane, right-click your account name.
3. Select **"Add shared folder or mailbox."**
4. Type the role mailbox address (e.g., `cscchair@sandiahomeowners.org`) and press Enter.
5. The shared mailbox should now appear under **"Shared with me"** in the folder pane.

**In the new Outlook desktop app** (Windows or Mac):

1. Open Outlook and go to **Mail**.
2. In the left folder pane, right-click your account name.
3. Select **"Add shared folder or mailbox."**
4. Type the role mailbox address and click **Add**.

**If it still does not appear**, your account may not have been granted permission to the shared mailbox yet. Contact **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** and ask them to verify your mailbox permissions.

> **Microsoft help:** [Open and use a shared mailbox in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/open-and-use-a-shared-mailbox-in-outlook-d94a8e9e-21f1-4240-808b-de9c9c088afd) — see the section "What if it didn't work?"

## Where should Board materials be submitted?

**Upload directly to the Board's SharePoint site** using the direct link provided in your Board materials or by ITHelp.

**Step by step:**

1. Open the Board SharePoint link in your web browser (you do not need to install anything).
2. Navigate to the appropriate folder (e.g., "Meeting Materials", "Agendas", etc.).
3. Click **Upload** → **Files** and select the document from your computer.
4. Once uploaded, copy the sharing link (see the file-sharing steps above) and email it to the Board mailing list if you need to notify members.

Only deviate from this process if office staff specifically requests you to submit materials a different way (e.g., emailing the document directly to them).

## Why am I not getting Board reminder emails?

Board meeting reminders are sent automatically from a **shared Google Calendar** workflow, not from Microsoft 365. Common reasons you might not be receiving them:

- **Check your Spam / Junk folder.** Automated calendar reminders often get filtered.
- **Verify your email address is correct** in the Google Calendar sharing settings. If you recently changed your email, the reminders may be going to your old address.
- **Confirm you accepted the Google Calendar invitation.** You may have received a separate calendar-sharing email from Google that needs to be accepted.

If none of the above resolves it, contact **office staff** or **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** to have them verify you are included in the Board calendar.

**It depends on your role.**

You do not need to log in to anything special to read committee emails — they arrive in your normal inbox.

**Not sure which category you fall into?** Ask your committee chair or email **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org**.

## Can I forward my SHHA role mailbox email to my personal email?

**This is not recommended, and you cannot set it up yourself.**

Role mailboxes (e.g., `president@sandiahomeowners.org`) are shared inboxes managed by SHHA's Microsoft 365 administrator. Only an admin can configure forwarding. Forwarding is generally discouraged because:

- Replies sent from your personal inbox will not appear in the role mailbox, breaking the continuity of records for future officers.
- Forwarded copies in your personal inbox will persist after you leave the role, which is a records-management concern.

**What to do instead:**

- **Check the role mailbox regularly** at [outlook.office.com](https://outlook.office.com). You can bookmark it for quick access.
- **Turn on notifications** in Outlook on the web: click the **gear icon** (⚙) in the top-right corner → **View all Outlook settings** → **General** → **Notifications** → enable email notifications. This way you get a browser notification when new mail arrives in the shared mailbox.
- **Use the Outlook mobile app** ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/microsoft-outlook/id951937596) / [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.office.outlook)): sign in with your `@sandiahomeowners.org` account, and the shared mailbox can be added there too.

If you have an exceptional need for forwarding, contact **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** to discuss options.

> **Microsoft help:** [Add a shared mailbox to Outlook mobile](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/add-a-shared-mailbox-to-outlook-mobile-f866242c-81b2-472e-8776-6c49c5473c9f)

**For regular committee email (if you are an external volunteer):** Committee emails are delivered directly to your personal inbox. No special setup is needed — they arrive wherever you normally read email.

## How do I see emails going between committee or Board members? Can I see older emails from before I joined?

**Committee mailing list emails:**

- When someone sends a message to a committee group address (e.g., `csc@sandiahomeowners.org`), every current member of that group receives a copy in their own inbox.
- You can see any email sent to the group **from the date you were added** onward. Emails arrive in your personal inbox (for external volunteers) or your SHHA inbox (for licensed users).
- **You cannot see emails from before you were added** to the mailing list. Microsoft Groups deliver to current members only; there is no self-service web archive for external members.

**If you need to see prior committee email history** (for example, to get up to speed when joining a committee mid-term):

- Ask your **committee chair** or a current member to forward key threads or a summary.
- Check the committee's **SharePoint document library** — important decisions and documents should be saved there as files, not just buried in email.
- The SHHA **archive mailbox** (`itadmin@sandiahomeowners.org`) retains a copy of all group emails. If significant prior history is needed, contact **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** and they can retrieve specific messages.

**Shared role mailbox emails:**

- If you hold a role (e.g., committee chair, President), the shared role mailbox **retains its full email history** regardless of who currently has access. When you are granted access to a role mailbox, you can scroll back through all prior sent and received messages. This is one of the major advantages of using role mailboxes for SHHA business.

## How does the email transition work when someone enters or leaves a role? Who handles it?

**This is NOT automatic.** A human (office staff) must update group memberships and mailbox permissions.

**Here is how it works, from the officer/volunteer perspective:**

### When you are the incoming person (new to a role):

1. **Your committee chair** (or the Board, for executive roles) notifies **office staff** that you are taking over.
2. **Office staff** (currently Anna) adds you to the appropriate committee mailing list(s) and, if the role has a shared mailbox, requests that IT grant you access.
3. **ITHelp** grants you permission to the shared role mailbox (e.g., `cscchair@sandiahomeowners.org`).
4. You will then be able to open the shared mailbox at [outlook.office.com](https://outlook.office.com) and see the full email history from prior role holders.
5. You will begin receiving all committee group emails at your email address.

### When you are the outgoing person (leaving a role):

1. Notify your committee chair and office staff of your departure date.
2. **Office staff** removes you from the committee mailing list(s). You will stop receiving group emails.
3. **ITHelp** removes your access to the shared role mailbox. You will no longer be able to open it.
4. The shared mailbox itself (and all its email history) remains intact for your successor.

### Overlap period:

- There is **no automatic overlap**. If you want your successor to start seeing emails before your access is removed, ask office staff to add them to the group and grant mailbox access while you are still active.
- It is good practice to briefly **overlap** so the new person can ask questions while the outgoing person is still receiving messages.

### Who does what:

| Action | Who handles it |
|---|---|
| Notify staff of the change | Outgoing or incoming person, or committee chair |
| Add/remove people from committee mailing lists | **Office staff** (Anna) |
| Grant/revoke shared mailbox access | **ITHelp / IT admin** |
| Preserve email history in role mailbox | Automatic — the mailbox retains everything |

**Important for the President and executive officers:** You do not need to know the technical steps for granting or revoking access — that is handled by staff and ITHelp. Your responsibility is to **make sure the transition is communicated** to office staff promptly so they can initiate the changes.

> *Note: The instructions above are for officers and volunteers who need to **use** the system. Staff who need to **administer** these changes should refer to the [Staff](staff.html) page and the [SHHA Microsoft 365 Email Groups & Roles](shha-microsoft-365-email-groups-roles.html) reference document.*

# Finding Phone Numbers and Contact Information

## How do I find phone numbers for Board, committee, and staff members?

**There is no centralized online directory with phone numbers** in the Microsoft 365 system. Most SHHA members do not list personal phone numbers in the community directory. However, there are a few ways to get contact information when you need it:

1. **Ask office staff.** Email **office@sandiahomeowners.org** (or your usual staff contact) and request the phone number(s) you need. Staff maintain internal contact lists that are not published online.
2. **Ask your committee chair.** Chairs typically maintain a roster of their committee members with phone numbers shared privately.
3. **Check the SHHA community directory** (if available). Some members do list phone numbers there. The directory may be available on the SHHA website or as a printed/PDF document distributed to residents.
4. **Board and executive contacts.** If you are a Board member or officer, you should receive a Board contact list (including phone numbers) at the beginning of your term. If you have not received one, ask the President or office staff.

**For emergencies:** If you urgently need to reach a Board or staff member and cannot get a phone number through the above channels, email **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** and **office@sandiahomeowners.org** simultaneously, mark the subject as **URGENT**, and explain the situation. Staff can relay messages. 

**Suggestion for Board and committee chairs:** Maintain a simple phone/email contact sheet for your group members and share it privately (not on SharePoint) at the start of each term so everyone has emergency contact information.

## How do I get into committee or Board SharePoint folders?

SharePoint is where SHHA stores committee documents, meeting materials, and work products. Each committee has its own SharePoint site with document libraries (folders).

### Getting to SharePoint for the first time:

1. **Use the direct link** provided by your committee chair, office staff, or ITHelp. This is the easiest and most reliable method. The link will look something like:
   `https://sandiahomeowners.sharepoint.com/sites/YourCommitteeName`
2. **Click the link** in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge — any browser works).
3. **Sign in** when prompted:
   - If you are an **external volunteer**, sign in with the personal email address that received your Microsoft invitation.
   - If you have an **`@sandiahomeowners.org` account**, sign in with that.
4. You should now see the committee's SharePoint site with its document library.

### If you do not have a direct link:

- Try browsing to [sandiahomeowners.sharepoint.com](https://sandiahomeowners.sharepoint.com) and looking for your committee's site.
- Ask your committee chair to send you the link.
- Email **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** and ask for the SharePoint URL for your committee.

### Which SharePoint sites can I access?

You can only access the SharePoint sites you have been specifically granted access to. Typically:
- You can access your **own committee's** SharePoint site.
- You **cannot** access other committees' SharePoint sites unless you are also a member of those committees or have been specifically granted access.
- **Board members** may have access to the Board SharePoint site and potentially other committee sites depending on their role.

If you believe you should have access to a site but are getting an "Access Denied" error, contact **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org**.

### How to navigate within a SharePoint site:

Once you are on your committee's SharePoint site:

1. Click **Documents** in the left sidebar (or at the top of the page) to see the document library.
2. Click on **folder names** to navigate deeper into subfolders.
3. Use the **breadcrumb trail** at the top of the page (e.g., `Documents > 2026 > Meeting Materials`) to navigate back up.

### How to upload a file:

1. Navigate to the folder where the file should go.
2. Click **Upload** → **Files** (in the toolbar at the top).
3. Select the file from your computer and click **Open**.
4. The file will appear in the library. You can then share a link to it (see the [file sharing FAQ](#can-i-share-a-file-directly-to-the-committee-mailing-list-from-sharepoint) above).

### How to download a file:

1. Navigate to the file in SharePoint.
2. Click on the **file name** to open it in the browser. From there, click **Download** (or the download icon) near the top of the page.
3. **Alternatively**, select the file (click the circle icon next to its name) and then click the **Download** button in the toolbar at the top.

> **Microsoft help:** [Upload files and folders to a SharePoint document library](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/upload-files-and-folders-to-a-document-library-eb18fcba-c953-4d45-8d90-8da66edeacdb) and [Download files and folders from SharePoint](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/download-files-and-folders-from-onedrive-or-sharepoint-5c7397b7-19c7-4893-84fe-d02e8fa5df05)

## Where do I find minutes from committee or Board meetings?

Meeting minutes are typically stored in the committee's **SharePoint document library**.

**To find them:**

1. Open your committee's **SharePoint site** (use the direct link from your chair, or see the [SharePoint access section](#how-do-i-get-into-committee-or-board-sharepoint-folders) above).
2. Click **Documents** in the left sidebar.

**If minutes are not in SharePoint:**
- Some committees may not have digitized their minutes yet or may store them differently. Ask your committee chair where minutes are kept.
- Board meeting minutes may also be posted on the **SHHA website** at [sandiahomeowners.org](https://sandiahomeowners.org) once approved, since approved Board minutes are typically shared with the community.

**If you cannot find minutes for a specific meeting**, email your committee chair or **office staff** to ask.

## How do I submit an article for The GRIT newsletter?

The GRIT is SHHA's community newsletter. Articles are submitted to the GRIT editor (part of the Communication & Publication Committee) on a monthly cycle.

### President's Letter:

- **Frequency:** Monthly — one letter per GRIT issue.
- **Word count:** Typically **300–500 words** (check with the GRIT editor for the current guideline, as it may vary by issue).
- **How to submit:** Email your article as a **Word document (.docx)** or in the **body of the email** to the GRIT editor. Ask the Communication & Publication Committee chair or office staff for the current editor's email address if you do not have it.
- **Deadline:** Usually **mid-month** for the following month's issue (e.g., mid-February for the March issue). The exact deadline is set by the GRIT editor and announced to contributors. Contact the editor or office staff to confirm the deadline.
- **Images:** If you want to include a photo, attach a **high-resolution image** (JPEG or PNG) as a separate file — do not embed it in the Word document. Include a caption if needed.

### Other articles (committee reports, event announcements, feature stories):

- **Who can submit:** Any Board member, committee chair, or authorized volunteer. If you are not sure whether you can submit an article, ask your committee chair.
- **Word count:** Varies by article type. Short announcements may be 100–200 words; longer features can be 500+ words. Check with the GRIT editor.
- **How to submit:** Same process — email the article to the GRIT editor as a Word document or in the email body, along with any images as separate attachments.
- **Deadline:** Same monthly deadline as the President's Letter.

### General tips for GRIT submissions:

- **Submit early.** Late submissions may be held for the next issue.
- **Proofread** your article before submitting. The editor may do light editing but may not catch everything.
- **Include a headline/title** for your article.
- **Provide photo credits** if images are not your own.

If you are not sure who the current GRIT editor is, email **office@sandiahomeowners.org** or your committee chair.

> *For the layout/design process (used by the GRIT editor, not article submitters), see the [GRIT Layout Monthly Guide](grit-layout.html).*

## How do I send an email blast to all SHHA residents?

**You cannot send an email blast to residents yourself.** Email blasts to the full SHHA membership are sent by **office staff** using SHHA's bulk email system.

**How to request an email blast:**

1. **Draft your message.** Write the content you want sent, including any links or images. Keep it clear and concise.
2. **Email your draft to office staff.** Send it to **office@sandiahomeowners.org** (or your primary staff contact) with a subject line like *"Email blast request — [topic]"*.
3. **Include the target audience.** Specify who should receive it — all residents, a specific neighborhood section, etc.
4. **Include your desired send date.** Give staff at least a few business days lead time.
5. **Staff will review and send.** Office staff format the message for the bulk email system and distribute it.

**Who has oversight over email blasts?**

- **Office staff** are the gatekeepers. They control what goes out through the bulk email system.
- **Board approval** may be required for certain messages, depending on SHHA policy. If you are unsure whether your message needs Board approval, ask the President or office manager.
- Routine operational messages (meeting reminders, committee announcements) typically do not need separate Board approval.
- Policy statements, financial updates, or messages on behalf of the Board generally do need approval.

**Important:** Do not attempt to send mass emails by putting all resident email addresses in the To or CC field of a regular email. This exposes everyone's email address, often triggers spam filters, and is not the proper SHHA process.

## When should I contact IT Help?

Contact **ITHelp@sandiahomeowners.org** if:

- You **cannot access SharePoint** — you get an "Access Denied" or "You need permission" error
- You are **not receiving committee emails** at your personal address
- You **never received or cannot find** your original Microsoft invitation email
- You **cannot see a shared role inbox** in Outlook after trying the steps above
- You are **unsure which email address** to use for SHHA
- You are **stuck trying to share a file** and the steps in this FAQ did not resolve it
- You need your **invitation resent** because the original link expired
- You are seeing **unexpected Microsoft prompts** (e.g., requests to join Teams, license warnings) that you do not understand
- You need **prior email history** retrieved from the archive mailbox
- You need to set up or change **role mailbox access** during an officer transition
- You cannot find the **SharePoint link** for your committee

**Contact office staff** (**office@sandiahomeowners.org**) if:

- You need someone **added to or removed from** a committee mailing list
- You need a **phone number** or contact information for a Board/committee/staff member
- You want to submit a **GRIT article** but do not know the current editor's email
- You want to request an **email blast** to residents
- You need to report a **leadership transition** (new officer or chair) so access can be updated

**Needing help is completely normal.** The Microsoft 365 guest system can be confusing, especially if you have not used it before. ITHelp and office staff are there to sort it out.

**When you email ITHelp, include:**
- Your **full name**
- The **email address** you use for SHHA
- A **screenshot** of any error message (if possible)
- A brief description of **what you were trying to do** and **what happened instead**

This helps resolve your issue faster.
